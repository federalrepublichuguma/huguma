FEDERAL GOVERNMENT

Council of Ministers

The Federal Government of Huguma consists of the President, Prime Minister and federal ministers. Each minister is responsible for a specific portfolio and assists in the administration of the Republic.

Minister of Health

Azuwairie

The Minister of Health is responsible for health affairs and public health administration within the Federal Republic of Huguma.

The ministry develops health policies, coordinates health-related programmes and advises the federal government on matters concerning public health.

Minister of Economy

Amni Ufairah

The Minister of Economy is responsible for the economic affairs and financial development of the Federal Republic of Huguma.

The ministry oversees economic policy, government finances, budgeting and initiatives supporting sustainable economic development.

Minister of Development and Infrastructure

Ammar Ziqrie

The Minister of Development and Infrastructure is responsible for national development and infrastructure affairs.

The ministry oversees development initiatives, public facilities, infrastructure projects and proposals intended to improve Huguma's administered areas.

Minister of Foreign Affairs

Nooby Taisoech

The Minister of Foreign Affairs is responsible for the foreign relations and diplomatic affairs of the Federal Republic of Huguma.

The ministry manages diplomatic relations, international communications, treaties, international organisations and foreign policy matters.

Minister of Technology

Abdul Moeez

The Minister of Technology is responsible for technology, digital government and technological development within Huguma.

The ministry supports digital government initiatives, technological innovation and the development of online national services.

FEDERAL EXECUTIVE

Role of the Ministers

Federal ministers administer their respective portfolios and support the President and Prime Minister in the operation of the Federal Government.

Federal Government

Ministers are appointed to lead specific areas of national administration. They are responsible for developing policies, overseeing government programmes and advising the President and Prime Minister on matters within their portfolios.

The President serves as the principal executive authority, while the Prime Minister serves as Head of Government and coordinates the work of the federal government and its ministers.
