OUR REPUBLIC

The Federal Republic of Huguma

The Federal Republic of Huguma is a digital micronational federal republic founded upon the principles of unity, responsibility, peace and innovation. Huguma develops a structured national community through government institutions, citizenship and diplomacy.

Huguma seeks peaceful cooperation with communities around the world while building a modern digital republic for its citizens.

OUR HISTORY

Foundation of Huguma

Huguma was established as a community-driven republic with the goal of creating a peaceful and organised micronational society.

Since its foundation, Huguma has focused on developing government institutions, E-Citizenship, diplomacy and national development programmes for the future.
