THE REPUBLIC

Federal Republic of Huguma

A digital federal republic committed to community, innovation, sustainability and peaceful international relations.

Our Republic

The Federal Republic of Huguma is organised as a federal republic with national institutions and an executive government.

Huguma seeks to develop a responsible and sustainable digital community while building peaceful relationships with communities around the world.

The Republic's capital is Bandar Mewar.
