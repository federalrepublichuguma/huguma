About Jemaah EXCO

Jemaah EXCO is the executive council of the Federal Republic of Huguma.

Executive Administration

The Jemaah EXCO works alongside the President, Vice President, Prime Minister and Vice Prime Minister in developing the Republic.

Each EXCO is responsible for a specific area of public administration and national development.

The council supports the implementation of HUGUMA NAHDAH 2034 and the long-term development of the Republic.
