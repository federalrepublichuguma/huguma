FEDERAL EXECUTIVE COUNCIL

Jemaah EXCO

Allah Bersama Huguma

The Jemaah EXCO forms part of the executive government of the Federal Republic of Huguma. Each EXCO is responsible for a specific area of national administration.

EXECUTIVE COUNCIL

Jemaah EXCO of Huguma

The Jemaah EXCO assists the federal government in administering the affairs of the Republic.

Diplomatic EXCO

Spooderman

The Diplomatic EXCO, currently held by Spooderman, is responsible for assisting in the conduct of Huguma's foreign relations.

Its responsibilities include communication with foreign entities, diplomatic missions, treaties, international organisations and other diplomatic activities.

Justice EXCO

Pope Benedict

The Justice EXCO, currently held by Pope Benedict, is responsible for legal and justice-related affairs within the executive government.

The office advises the Executive Council on legal matters, assists with the preparation and review of legislation and contributes to the development of Huguma's legal framework.

Pope Benedict concurrently serves as the Supreme Judge of the Federal Republic.

The two offices are institutionally distinct: the Justice EXCO forms part of the Executive Council, while the Supreme Judge is the head of the judiciary.

Defense EXCO

Daor Sacul

The Defense EXCO, currently held by Daor Sacul, is responsible for defence-related affairs and the administration of Huguma's defence institutions.

The office also advises the federal government on matters relating to security, preparedness and the protection of government institutions.

Development EXCO

Nooby

The Development EXCO, currently held by Nooby, is responsible for matters relating to development and infrastructure in Huguma.

The office oversees development initiatives, public facilities and proposals concerning the improvement of areas administered by the government.

Sports EXCO

Owenn

The Sports EXCO, currently held by Owenn, is responsible for sporting and recreational affairs.

The office coordinates sporting activities and competitions and promotes participation in sports among Huguman citizens.

Finance EXCO

Amni Ufairah

The Finance EXCO, currently held by Amni Ufairah, is responsible for the financial affairs of the federal government.

Its responsibilities include budgeting, maintaining financial records, managing government expenditure and advising the government on financial policy.

Immigrations EXCO

Maki

The Immigrations EXCO, currently held by Maki, is responsible for immigration and residency affairs.

The office administers immigration procedures and records and assists with matters concerning entry, residency and related applications under Huguman law.
