INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

Foreign Affairs of Huguma

Huguma seeks to maintain respectful and peaceful relations through dialogue, mutual recognition, cooperation and diplomatic engagement.

Peaceful Diplomacy

The Federal Republic of Huguma supports constructive dialogue and friendly relations with micronations and communities around the world.

Diplomatic relations may include mutual recognition, formal communications, treaties, consular relations, international cooperation and participation in intermicronational organisations.

Foreign affairs are coordinated by the Minister of Foreign Affairs on behalf of the Federal Government.
