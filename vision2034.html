NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT PLAN

HUGUMA NAHDAH 2034

Towards a Strong, Sustainable & Connected Republic. A long-term national vision for the development of the Federal Republic of Huguma through community, innovation, sustainability and international friendship.

OUR NATIONAL VISION

Huguma Nahdah 2034

HUGUMA NAHDAH 2034 is the Republic's long-term development vision towards the year 2034.

Building the Future

Huguma seeks to build a strong, sustainable and connected digital republic.

The vision focuses on growing the E-Citizen community, developing a sustainable economy, strengthening government institutions and expanding international diplomacy.

NATIONAL TARGETS

Five Priorities for 2034

01

500 E-Citizens

Grow Huguma's registered E-Citizen community to 500 people by 2034.

02

Sustainable Economy

Encourage a responsible digital economy based on innovation, trade and sustainable development.

03

Modern Government

Develop stronger institutions, digital public services and transparent administration.

04

Global Diplomacy

Establish peaceful diplomatic relations and partnerships with communities worldwide.

05

Presidential Whitehouse

Develop the Presidential Whitehouse in Bandar Mewar as the official presidential residence and national symbol.
